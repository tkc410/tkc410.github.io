What I do

I take hard decisioning problems in regulated, high-volume environments and turn them into AI systems that hold up in production: retrieval and semantic search, model selection and routing, guardrails, human-in-the-loop design, evaluation harnesses, LLMOps and observability, on a cloud, Kubernetes and SRE foundation I spent a decade building. The pattern repeats. Frame the problem, prove it with my own hands at small scale, set the design standards, then build out the 30-plus-person global organization across three geographies that carries it the rest of the way. The compliance AI program at eBay went from three engineers and a proof of concept to every listing on the site that way.

The hard part is rarely the model. It is the trade-offs around it: accuracy against inference cost, speed against auditability, automation against a person's right to be reviewed, and which of those a team should own versus buy.
